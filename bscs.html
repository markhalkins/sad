About Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Learn what makes our BSCS program exceptional for aspiring computer scientists:

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at North Negros College is a comprehensive 4-year program designed to prepare students for successful careers in technology and software development. Our curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with practical hands-on experience.

What You'll Learn

  • Programming Languages: Java, Python, C++, JavaScript, and more
  • Data Structures & Algorithms: Master complex problem-solving techniques
  • Database Management: SQL, database design, and management systems
  • Web Development: Full-stack development with modern frameworks
  • Software Engineering: Project management and software development lifecycle
  • Cybersecurity: Network security and information protection

Career Opportunities

Graduates of our BSCS program pursue rewarding careers as:

  • Software Developer
  • Systems Analyst
  • Network Administrator
  • Database Administrator
  • Cybersecurity Specialist
  • IT Consultant
